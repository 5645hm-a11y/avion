### Motion accuracy against ground truth (2026-11-04)

The speed row above compares AVION's 256 tile vectors with dense DIS, which computes far more than that. A reviewer pointed this
out, correctly (2026-11-04). The like-for-like baseline is OpenCV's sparse pyramidal Lucas-Kanade at the same 256 tile centres.
Speed means little without accuracy, so here is the endpoint error against dense ground truth for every method.
`tools/realworld/eval_flow.py`; `media_eval_flow` (`tools/eval_flow.cpp`); the dense methods are averaged over each tile.

- **Ground truth:** the mean of the dense ground-truth flow over each tile's non-occluded pixels. A tile less than half visible is
  skipped.
- **EPE own:** a method's error on the tiles it calls valid. **EPE common:** its error on the tiles every method calls valid.
- **Time:** one core, median per picture pair. AVION's includes time to contact, saliency and objects.
- **`AVION + FlowRefiner`:** `video::FlowRefiner` (API 15): single-level 21×21 Lucas-Kanade at full resolution, started from the
  grid's answer.

**MPI Sintel, training, clean pass** (1,041 pairs, 1024×436; 254,942 tiles with ground truth, 116,611 valid for all; run again
2026-11-05 with the coarse level of API 17 as its own rows):

**The coarse level (2026-11-05, API 17; `video::Perception::SetPyramid`, off by default).** The directive was a coarse-to-fine
pyramid for motion beyond the search. Built and measured: a 32×32 coarse grid per picture of the history (each cell the mean of
4×4 cells), the tile's 4×4 coarse cells matched over ±5 coarse cells (four times the fine window's reach; every second
position, then the 3×3 around the cheapest; SSE2/AVX2 with a bit-identical scalar twin), and its answer used as a seed and as a
second fine window when the first did not settle. It reports more of the fast tiles (32+ px a frame: 9.2 % to 14.4 %). Their
error stays that of no motion (49.2 to 48.1 px), its own tiles' error rises (2.41 to 2.73) and it costs about 110 µs a pair.
Four other variants were measured and dropped:

- re-centring the window on the coarse answer unconditionally (wrong far matches on textured test scenes);
- a 10 % margin for the far window (two salient tiles too many under shake);
- measuring fast tiles over one picture interval when the coarse level says the motion is beyond reach (32+ px 50.5 → 39.9,
  but 0-2 px 0.58 → 1.23: the trigger fires on slow tiles);
- ±8 coarse cells (48.1 → 51.2 at a wider window, no better).

The tiles that fail are not object edges: on tiles whose ground truth is uniform (spread under a fifth of the motion), the
error is the same (48.8 px). So fast motion stays AVION's failure, as measured.

**Why the coarse level fails (traced 2026-11-05).** `media_flow_trace` is `media_eval_flow` with its own copy of
`Perception.cpp` built with `MEDIA_PERCEPTION_TRACE`. It records every main tile's search stage by stage: the baseline, the fine
window's answer and whether it settled, whether the coarse level ran, its answer or its reason for refusing, and the re-centred
window. `tools/realworld/trace_flow.py` joins the trace with the ground truth for the 21,575 tiles of 32+ px a frame whose
truth is one uniform motion (Sintel clean, all 23 sequences):

- **The baseline triples the motion.** Perception measures over the newest picture at least `kBaseline` (90 ms) old. At
  Sintel's 24 fps that is three pictures back (125 ms) for 96 % of these tiles, so a motion of 32 px a frame is 96 px to find.
- **The coarse window is too short for it, vertically above all.** The grid is 128 cells a side whatever the picture's shape:
  a cell is 8 x 3.4 px at 1024 x 436, a coarse cell 32 x 13.6 px. The window's interior (±4.5 coarse cells; a minimum on its
  edge is refused) reaches ±144 px across and ±61 px up and down over three pictures: 48 px a frame across, **20 px a frame
  vertically**. For 76 % of the tiles (16,426) the truth lies outside the window. The radius was never the limit across; the
  baseline and the cell's height were.
- **Inside the window the match is not distinct.** Of the 5,149 tiles whose truth the window holds, the coarse level refuses
  58 % (2,998), mostly with no clear minimum (the best cost is not below half the mean of the 36 lattice positions). It answers
  wrongly for 18 % (910) and rightly for 12 % (some 600, of which 395 then find nothing in the re-centred fine window). A 4 x 4
  block of means of 32 x 13.6 px cells, three pictures apart, does not single out one position on Sintel's deforming
  characters. Aliasing is not the cause: each coarse cell is the mean of 4 x 4 cells, a box filter before the decimation.
- **Outcome:** 67 % refused, 23 % a wrong answer, 4.8 % solved (error under 8 px), 1 % a fine window that settled on a wrong
  match before the coarse level could run.

The fix the trace points to was measured: when the long baseline finds nothing and the coarse level ran, search the picture
before (a third of the displacement, inside the window), and keep that answer only for a motion the long baseline could not
reach, so slow tiles keep theirs (`Perception::SetShortBaseline`, opt-in). It doubles what the coarse level reports of the
fast tiles (coverage at 32+ px 14.4 % to 28.9 %) and leaves slow motion exactly as it was (0-2 px 0.60, 2-8 px 1.26 in both
runs). But half of the new answers are wrong: the error on the tiles AVION reports rises from 2.73 to 3.33 px, and the 32+ px
error falls only from 51.6 to 50.5 px (uniform tiles 51.3 to 50.0; solved tiles 4.8 % to 15.2 % in the trace). That is not
a clean solution of fast motion, so the coarse level and its short baseline stay off. Solving it needs a support that tells
positions apart at that scale (more coarse cells, or texture-aware matching), not more reach.

**Final pass** (motion blur, fog, depth of field; 94,868 tiles common): AVION + FlowRefiner 43.8 % coverage, EPE own 2.20, common
1.49, 0-2 px 0.34; LK 21×21 75.9 %, 6.05, 1.98, 0.68; DIS ultrafast 3.78, 0.76, 0.31; DIS medium 3.18, 0.52, 0.18.

**Middlebury "other"** (8 real-camera pairs, small motion; 1,363 common tiles): AVION 1.24 → 1.01 with the cell refinement tried
and dropped (it cost the 600 µs budget), 0.69 with FlowRefiner; LK 21×21 0.65; DIS ultrafast 0.58.

What this says:

- **Small motion (under 8 px per frame) is AVION's strength.** With FlowRefiner it is the most accurate sparse method measured here,
  and it matches dense DIS ultrafast below 2 px (0.26 against 0.27). It runs at 1.2 ms a pair against 5.8 ms for LK 21×21, 4.7x
  faster.
- **Large motion is a failure, not a trade-off.** Above 32 px per frame AVION's error (49 px) is that of answering "no motion"
  (55 px). LK's pyramid reaches 26 px and DIS 13 px. A coarse level added in API 17 (above) reports more of those tiles but does
  not make them right.
- **Coverage is half.** AVION reports 44-49 % of the tiles, LK 76-92 %. On Middlebury, LK was as accurate on the tiles AVION
  rejected as on the ones it kept (0.67 against 0.66), so AVION's texture test is stricter than the pictures need.
- **What AVION reports is reliable.** Its error on its own tiles is 2.0-2.2 px, against 5-6 px for LK on LK's own tiles. LK's
  status flag accepts many tiles it gets wrong.
- **Dense DIS is more accurate overall** (0.44-0.76 px) at 5-45 times the time. AVION does not replace it where a per-pixel field
  is needed.

## What these numbers do not say

- **Scan rows** compare reading slice headers with decoding pixels. That is the purpose of pre-decode inspection, not a like-for-like race between decoders.
- **Cut accuracy is a sanity check on two files**, scored against `scdet`'s cut times (as frame numbers; a cut within 4 frames counts, the inspector working in decode order). LG's reel: AVION {{frames(LG['avion_cuts'])}}; `scdet` {{frames(LG['scdet_cuts'])}}; found {{LG['cuts_matched']}}, missed {{frames(LG['cuts_missed'])}}, extra {{frames(LG['cuts_extra'])}}. The 720p film: AVION {{frames(CR['avion_cuts'])}}; `scdet` {{frames(CR['scdet_cuts'])}}; found {{CR['cuts_matched']}}, missed {{frames(CR['cuts_missed'])}}, extra {{frames(CR['cuts_extra'])}}. On {{P0['date']}} the inspector confirmed {{P0['scan']['lg_black_4k60_hdr.mp4']['avion_confirmed_cuts']}} cuts on LG's reel; why, and the fix, are in docs/picture.md ("On a real reel").
- **The motion speed row** compares AVION's tile flow (256 vectors a picture, plus time to contact, saliency and objects) with dense per-pixel flow. "Motion accuracy" above has the like-for-like comparison (sparse LK at the same points) and the errors.
- **WSL2** runs in a virtual machine on the same CPU; its file reads go through the WSL disk, so inputs were copied there first.
- One machine, one run per configuration (survey: best of 5 passes). Expect a few percent of run-to-run variation.
